Synopsis

Because of the chaos he has caused in his former life, Wei Wuxian has been hated, betrayed and was ultimately killed by the biggest clans. Many years later, a lunatic, expelled from his clan because of his homosexuality, performs a ritual causing Wei Wuxian’s soul to enter his body. In his new body, Wei Wuxian meets old acquaintances, including his arch-enemy Lan Wangji. Wei Wuxian realises that there’s more behind the cold and supercilious façade of his former nemesis than initially expected.

Together they have to face exciting and nerve-wrecking challenges, while the past keeps chasing them. This series focuses on the latter aspect.
